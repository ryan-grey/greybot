Welcome messages

Welcome the joining member with an @mention, their name and the waving-lizard GIF. Only that member can be pinged by this post.

Automatic moderation

Across channels, the fourth message within five seconds is deleted and records a private spam infraction. The third identical text within five seconds is deleted without an infraction unless it also exceeds the spam limit. No warning messages or DMs are sent.

Three spam infractions within 30 minutes trigger a mute until an admin revokes it. Revoking a mute resets the escalation count. Bots, webhooks, administrators and members at or above greyBot's role are excluded.

Member departures

Post the departed member's name and coffin-dance GIF. Applies to departures, including kicks and bans; Discord's member-left event does not identify the cause.
